History
of the Guitar

THE GENESIS (1950s)

The Birth of Solid Body Resonance.

The 1950s marked a pivotal shift from acoustic chambering to solid-body electric power. Innovators like Leo Fender and Ted McCarty transformed timber and electromagnetic pickups into instruments capable of filling stadiums.

These pioneering instruments gave birth to Rock & Roll, Blues, and modern recording techniques that still define tone standards today.

CRAFTSMANSHIP & TONESHAPING

Wood, Magnetics & Pure Expression.

Each instrument in this archive represents thousands of hours of hand-carved artistry. From Brazilian Rosewood fingerboards to hand-wound Alnico magnets, every nuance shapes human emotion into soundwaves.
